About Me

I'm a Geomatic Engineering graduate from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT, 2025) with hands-on experience in GIS, remote sensing, and land surveying. My work applies Python-based geospatial analysis to real-world environmental and infrastructure projects, translating field survey and satellite data into actionable maps and reports.

I'm a published co-author in two peer-reviewed journals on remote sensing and machine learning applications in forest ecosystem monitoring, and I bring a collaborative, solutions-driven approach to every geospatial output I deliver.
